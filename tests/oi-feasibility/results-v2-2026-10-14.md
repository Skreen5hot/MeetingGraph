## Findings

1. **One question per item works.** The invitations item is now `answered` in every run.
2. **Rule wording doesn't hold the model to rules 1 and 6.** Duplicate date flags fell from 2 runs to 1. `match` on the owner item didn't change: 2 runs of 3, the same as v1. Both are harmless, because the validator ignores `match` outside who-is items and the build suppresses a flag that duplicates an answered item. **Proposal:** treat both as handled by the build, not by the prompt. Keep C8 and C9 as diagnostics, not pass criteria.
3. **Existing workbench bug, found by this meeting (not caused by §16.16).**
   - A task owned by a person introduced in the same meeting never passes the acceptance check. The check compares the owner ref (`NEW-P1`) with the speaker's name (`Reyes, Morgan`), so it never matches.
   - So NEW-2 ("send James the badge pickup location", Morgan's own commitment at S017) is flagged TASK-NO-ACCEPTANCE in all six runs (v1 and v2).
   - In the three v2 runs the chat also classified Morgan's introduction (S002) as context. So Morgan's NewParty "owns no valid op" and is flagged PARTY-IDENTITY, and the review's default is to drop the person.
   - That would also drop the evidence behind the "Who is Morgan?" answer.
   - **Proposed fix (part of the §16.16 build):**
     - resolve `NEW-P` refs to the NewParty's name before the speaker check;
     - count a passing who-is answer as identity evidence.
4. **Minor:** some runs wrote segment notes like "Answered in S027." The coverage check calls these too thin (a repair item, not blocking). We can't tell from three runs whether the OPEN ITEMS framing causes this.

## Verdict

Feasibility holds: 12/12 clear links in v1 and in v2, with no false links. v2 fixed the two-part question. The other two findings are best handled in the build, with finding 3's fix included.
