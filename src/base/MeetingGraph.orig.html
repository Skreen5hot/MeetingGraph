    Merge duplicate entries (advanced, rarely needed)

    This is not how meetings are imported \u2014 for that, use Number segments above. Corrections merge duplicate register entries through the same gate: build a request, paste it into a new chat, paste the reply at Step 4 above. Nothing is deleted; the duplicate's ID becomes an alias of the survivor.

    Workspace

    Administration that is not part of any meeting. The register itself lives in the team folder; this browser only mirrors it (Open files, Export workspace and Clear are in the header bar).

    Out-of-meeting update (reviewer attestation)

    For facts you know outside any meeting — an emailed due-date confirmation, an ownership handoff accepted in the hallway. Write dated statements in your own name; your written statements are the evidence, and they go through the same gate as meetings: build the request, paste it into a new chat, then paste the reply at Step 4 on Process meeting. The register records “the reviewer states …” — never someone else's words as if said in a meeting.

    Initial setup — seed projects (one-time)

    Start the register from your project list (ID, title, description-status-blockers). Projects are created straight from the file. Work items, owners and blockers are then extracted in small batches with your enterprise LLM and checked here, the same way as meeting imports. Seeding only works before the first meeting import.
